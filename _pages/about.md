I am a Ph.D. student at [Peking University (PKU)](https://www.pku.edu.cn/), advised by [Cong Fang](https://congfang-ml.github.io/). Previously, I graduated and obtained my Bachelor’s degree from BJTU in 2023.

I am also a long-term research intern at [Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence (BIGAI)](https://www.bigai.ai/), affiliated with Machine Learning (ML) Lab and mentored by [Dr. Qing Li](https://liqing-ustc.github.io/).

My current research aims to build mathematical foundations for agents that can learn continually, adapt, and evolve. Concretely, I am interested in the learning dynamics of neural networks and large models under gradient descent, which I believe lie at the core of the plasticity–stability tradeoff. I am exploring Neural Tangent Kernel (NTK) theory and optimization theory to study these dynamics. In addition, I view reinforcement learning as a promising way for agents to self-improve beyond direct human supervision, and I am currently building continual-learning reinforcement learning agents.
