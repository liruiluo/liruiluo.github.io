I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Intelligence at [Peking University (PKU)](https://www.pku.edu.cn/), majoring in Computer Science and Technology (Intelligent Science and Technology), advised by [Cong Fang](https://congfang-ml.github.io/). I received my B.E. in Communication Engineering from the School of Electronic and Information Engineering at Beijing Jiaotong University (BJTU) in 2023.

I am currently an intern at [JD.com](https://www.jd.com/)'s Tech Genius Team (TGT, Top Young Technical Talent Program), working on Agentic RL post-training for e-commerce agents.

My research interests lie in building general-purpose coding agents that can complete long-horizon tasks and continually improve by learning from rewards obtained through interaction with long-horizon task environments. More specifically, I am interested in (1) agentic reinforcement learning for long-horizon tasks; (2) post-training agents to develop and use memory; (3) post-training agentic RL for recursive self-improvement (RSI), autoresearch, and machine learning engineering (MLE) agents; and (4) continual reinforcement learning over unbounded streams of tasks.
